Frequently asked

FAQ

What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. It is a system used to organize customer information, leads, sales activities and interactions in one place.

Do I need a CRM for my business?

If you're managing a growing number of leads or customers through spreadsheets, emails, WhatsApp or multiple disconnected tools, a CRM can help organize the process.

Can you connect my website to my CRM?

Yes. Website enquiry forms can be connected to a CRM so that new leads are automatically captured and organized.

Can you automate WhatsApp and email follow-ups?

Depending on the CRM and available integrations, automated notifications, emails and WhatsApp workflows can be implemented.

Can you work with my existing CRM?

Yes. If your existing CRM supports the required features and integrations, we can work with your current system rather than requiring you to start from scratch.

Will I need a new CRM?

Not necessarily. We can first understand your requirements and determine whether your existing tools are sufficient or whether another solution would be more suitable.
