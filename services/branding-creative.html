The fundamentals

What Makes A Strong Brand?

01 — Clear

Your visual identity should communicate what your business represents without unnecessary complexity.

02 — Memorable

Distinctive design elements help your business stand apart from competitors.

03 — Consistent

Your colors, typography, imagery and design style should work together across every platform.

04 — Relevant

Your brand should connect with the audience you actually want to reach.

05 — Flexible

Your identity should work equally well on a website, social media post, advertisement, business card or mobile screen.

06 — Professional

A polished visual identity can help build credibility and create a stronger first impression.

Frequently asked

FAQ

Do I need branding if I already have a logo?

Not necessarily. If you already have a good logo, we can build supporting brand elements such as colors, typography and visual guidelines around it.

What does a brand identity include?

Depending on your requirements, it can include your logo, color palette, typography, visual elements, imagery style and brand guidelines.

Can you design social media posts for my business?

Yes. We can create branded social media creatives that follow your established visual identity.

Can you create advertising creatives?

Yes. We can design creative assets for Meta Ads, Google campaigns, social media promotions and other digital marketing activities.

Can branding and website design be done together?

Yes. In fact, creating your brand identity before designing your website can help maintain a much more consistent visual experience.
